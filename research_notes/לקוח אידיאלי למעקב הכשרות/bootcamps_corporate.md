# Israeli bootcamps, cyber academies and corporate junior/graduate programs as customers for a "training cohort tracking + continue/drop decision room" product

Method note: web search worked (summaries only); WebFetch of innovationisrael.org.il failed (host not in the session's allowed domains), so no page was read in full. All findings below come from search-result summaries and should be re-verified at the source before the report relies on them. Date of research: 2026-11-18.

## How do these programs track trainees and decide continue / drop / hire? Which tools?

### Takeaway
I found NO public evidence on the internal tools (Salesforce, Monday, Moodle, Workday, spreadsheets) or on formal continue/drop decision processes at Hackeru, John Bryce, Sela, Naya, Appleseeds, Elevation, Tsofen or Basmach. The only evidence is generic: vendors sell cohort platforms and education CRMs, and Israeli SIS products are listed on Capterra Israel.

### Cited Findings
- Generic cohort/bootcamp software exists and is marketed globally: Disco (cohort platform / LMS for accelerators and bootcamps), OpenEduCat (bootcamp management), LeadSquared (CRM for coding bootcamps, per-cohort intake pipelines, seat tracking), RaftLabs (custom bootcamp platform development), Taskade (cohort progress dashboard template) — [Disco](https://www.disco.co/features/cohort-management-software); [OpenEduCat](https://openeducat.org/solutions-boot-camps-summer-schools/) (search returned locale variants, e.g. https://openeducat.org/fr/solutions-boot-camps-summer-schools/); [LeadSquared](https://www.leadsquared.com/us/education/crm-for-coding-bootcamps/); [RaftLabs](https://www.raftlabs.com/industries/coding-bootcamp); [Taskade](https://www.taskade.com/templates/education/cohort-progress-dashboard). These are vendor marketing pages; features are claims.
- Vendor-claimed price range for bootcamp/summer-school software: about $50-500/month for small programs, $5,000-50,000/year for larger ones — [OpenEduCat FAQ](https://openeducat.org/summer-school-management-software/); not independent, not Israel-specific.
- Israeli-listed student information / CRM products on Capterra Israel include SELMA SIS (aimed at vocational education providers), RIO Education (Salesforce EDA-based SIS), EduCore CRM, Success CRM — [SELMA SIS](https://www.capterra.co.il/software/1077152/SELMA-SIS); [Capterra IL SIS directory](https://www.capterra.co.il/directory/31032/student-information-system/software?page=5); [EduCore CRM](https://www.capterra.co.il/software/1102066/EduCore-CRM); [Success CRM](https://www.capterra.co.il/software/141715/success-crm). Listing only; no evidence which Israeli bootcamps use them.
- Salesforce Education Cloud is positioned as CRM + SIS for institutions — [Cloud4Good](https://cloud4good.com/?p=37042).

### Inferences
- Hypothesis: most Israeli academies run on a mix of a lead/enrollment CRM (Salesforce/HubSpot/Zoho/local CRM), an LMS or learning platform, and spreadsheets for per-trainee evaluation and continue/drop decisions. Not verified for any named institution.
- Hypothesis: continue/drop decisions are usually informal (instructor + program manager), because no source describes a formal written two-opinion process.

### Gaps
- No source found naming tools used by Hackeru, John Bryce, Sela, Naya, Appleseeds, Elevation, Tsofen, Basmach. Job ads / LinkedIn / customer-reference pages were not found via search; interviews would be needed.
- No evidence found on written continue/drop decision procedures or who owns them.

## Number of programs and trainees in Israel (with government funding)

### Takeaway
Government funding via the Innovation Authority is real and sizeable (NIS 54M to 49 entities aiming at ~12,000 trainees/placements; NIS 48M "Hazenek LaHitech" junior-practice track), but I found no consolidated count of programs or annual trainees in the 2025-26 window.

### Cited Findings
- 2018 Innovation Authority pilot: seven operators (including Appleseeds Academy and Elevation Academy) chosen from 24 bidders for state-funded coding bootcamps; first-year target ~280 trainees, cohort cap 30 per operator — [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-coding-boot-camps-for-state-1001239711); [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3739465,00.html); [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/operators-selected-to-run-boot-camps-to-teach-coding-amid-worker-pinch/). Dated (2018).
- Innovation Authority: NIS 54M to 49 entities to increase high-tech workforce, estimated ~12,000 employees trained and placed; Sela is listed (about 200 workers trained in its own program, mainly absorbed into Sela's software house) — [Innovation Authority press release](https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/press_release/nis-54-million-to-49-entities-to-increase-the-overall-number-of-employees-in-high-tech-industry/) (date not confirmed in the snippet).
- Elevation runs a program for job seekers from the eastern Negev (AI Implementer) and one with Atidna for the Arab population — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3913520,00.html); search summary of the Innovation Authority listing (https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/?p=23437).
- "Hazenek LaHitech" (practical training for juniors): call for companies and non-profits running practical training of at least 6 months (benefit track no. 44); Human Capital Fund clusters include placing juniors in industry, retraining academics, under-represented populations; grants of up to NIS 1M and NIS 1-5M tiers — [Innovation Authority call](https://innovationisrael.org.il/kol_kore/הזנק-להייטק-תכניות-להכשרה-מעשית/); [Human Capital Fund](https://innovationisrael.org.il/programs/קרן-ההון-האנושי-להייטק/).
- A 2024 Ice article: Hazenek junior-practice program total NIS 48M, ~NIS 19.5M from the Innovation Authority, potential ~1,000 graduates — [Ice](https://www.ice.co.il/finance/news/article/996999). Single secondary source.
- Migo (Haredi men): goal to train 3,000 in 3 years (per Innovation Authority page summary) — [Innovation Authority](https://innovationisrael.org.il/הזנק-להייטק-תוכניות-הכשרה-מעשית-לגו/).
- Appleseeds site claims "thousands of graduates a year" across all programs and ~82,000 beneficiaries/year, 75% placement in employment programs — [Appleseeds](https://appleseeds.org.il/en/). Self-reported, mixed scope.
- Cyber cohorts are small: Cyber4s second cohort 40 graduates (2021); JCT Cyber Elite first cohort 31, 2021 cohort 36; ThriveDX cyber impact bootcamp (Ministry of Economy backed) first cohort graduated May 2022; Equal Chance in Cyber two-month course — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3911495,00.html); [Calcalist JCT](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3921220,00.html); [PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/thrivedx-successfully-places-graduates-of-the-cybersecurity-impact-bootcamp-backed-by-israel-s-ministry-of-economy-813237519.html); [Algemeiner](https://www.algemeiner.com/?p=505723). Dated 2021-22.
- Tech labor context: tech employment about 405K in 2025 (+2.3%); tech job seekers peaked ~16.3K end of 2025 — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/calcalistech/article/s1cxkzgpr) (as summarized by search; verify).

### Inferences
- Cohort sizes of 20-40 and a few dozen to a few hundred trainees per operator suggest each program is small; the TAM comes from number of operators/employers (tens to low hundreds), not trainees.
- Hypothesis: grant-funded programs must report placement/attendance to funders, which creates a reporting pain a tracking tool could address; not verified.

### Gaps
- No total count of bootcamps/academies or annual trainees in Israel for 2025-26. Ministry of Labor vocational-training (Direct/"Kidum") numbers, Basmach and Tsofen figures not found. Tsofen and Basmach not surfaced by any search.
- Innovation Authority pages could not be fetched; dates/terms of current calls are unverified.

## Corporate graduate/junior/new-hire programs (banks, defense-tech, cyber)

### Takeaway
Junior intake is shrinking and selective; some employers run own cohorts, but I found nothing on bank programs or on their tracking/decision tooling.

### Cited Findings
- Only ~360 juniors entered the high-tech industry in a recent year vs ~6,500 relevant university graduates annually; junior positions fell by tens of percent; AI absorbing entry-level tasks — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rkmgrnwhfg) (via search summary; exact article/year to verify).
- Salesforce Israel's Futureforce recruits only university/college graduates, not bootcamp grads; Deloitte Israel recruiting 35 juniors for AI roles (of 70 hires) — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/bjv7kvglxe) (attribution of URL to this fact uncertain; verify).
- Sela runs an internal training program with Innovation Authority funding (see above).
- Hapoalim/Leumi/Discount graduate programs: search returned nothing relevant.

### Inferences
- Hypothesis: a corporate program that hires a cohort of ~20-70 and decides who is kept at the end is a natural fit for the "decision room", but they likely do it in the ATS/HRIS (Workday, SuccessFactors) plus manager discussion.

### Gaps
- No data on bank, defense-tech (Elbit, Rafael, IAI) or cyber-company (Check Point etc.) graduate programs, their size, owners or tools.

## Decision-maker, payer, sales cycle, spend, fast-buying sub-segment

### Takeaway
Not answerable from public sources. Only the vendor price range above and structure of funding are available.

### Cited Findings
- Funding flows through grants to operators (Innovation Authority Human Capital Fund; up to NIS 1M and 1-5M tiers; 50% Authority share cited for another sub-track) — [Innovation Authority](https://innovationisrael.org.il/programs/קרן-ההון-האנושי-להייטק/).
- Vendor price range $5K-50K/yr for larger programs — [OpenEduCat](https://openeducat.org/summer-school-management-software/).

### Inferences
- Hypothesis: buyer = program director/COO at an academy or HR/L&D head at a corporation; small non-profit programs may buy fast and cheap; enterprises have long procurement cycles. Unverified.

### Gaps
- No sources on sales cycles, budgets, or buyer titles in Israel. Interviews needed.

## Competitors and what they lack

### Takeaway
Generic cohort/LMS/CRM tools exist; none found that offer a formal two-opinion, single-authorizer decision log.

### Cited Findings
- Disco, OpenEduCat, LeadSquared, RaftLabs, Taskade, Salesforce Education Cloud/RIO Education, SELMA SIS (links above). Their pages describe progress dashboards, milestone alerts, admissions pipelines; none of the summaries mention written-opinion decision workflows or audit logs.

### Inferences
- Hypothesis: the gap is the structured continue/drop decision with required opinions and audit trail; also Hebrew/RTL local support. Should be validated by reading feature lists.

### Gaps
- No Capterra/G2 reviews read; no Israeli bootcamp-specific competitor found.

## Evidence of pain

### Takeaway
Weak, indirect.

### Cited Findings
- Attrition indicators are not bootcamp-specific: 25% of junior/management candidates drop out at the assignment stage at Wolt Market; 32% dropout in math/CS degrees — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/xgkjv7ipy) (via summary; verify); [Infinity Labs](https://infinitylabs.co.il/dropping-out-of-computer-science-studies-lets-talk-about-it/).
- A nisha.co.il post claims 87% of certain bootcamp graduates enter hi-tech but only 4.5% of hi-tech roles are for those with no experience — [nisha](https://www.nisha.co.il/?p=11705); unofficial, unverified.

### Inferences
- Hypothesis: shrinking junior market raises the stakes of each retain/drop decision and funder accountability, but no source states that programs struggle with decision documentation.

### Gaps
- No direct evidence (complaints, disputes over dismissals, lawsuits, interviews) of pain in decision-making or tracking for these programs.
